TrustLens.ai Digital Payment Verification

TrustLens is a multi-modal artificial intelligence system engineered to expose forged and templated UPI payment receipts. Powered by MobileNetV2 visual deep learning, EasyOCR semantic entity extraction, and XGBoost classification with SHAP forensic explainability.

Core Technologies

  • EasyOCR Entity Recognizer
  • Multi-Modal Feature Fusion
  • XGBoost Fraud Classifier
  • SHAP Explainable AI (XAI)
